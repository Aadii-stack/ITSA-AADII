# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Current and prospective engineering students in the Department of Information Technology at Shree L. R. Tiwari College of Engineering (SLRTCE). Visitors arrive to discover upcoming technical events, workshops, hackathons, flagship tech summits, peer mentorship programs, and ways to join or collaborate with the student association committee.

## Product Purpose

The official digital hub for ITSA (Information Technology Student Association) at SLRTCE. The platform exists to cultivate student innovation, hands-on technical excellence, and active peer collaboration. Success means students discover, engage with, and register for technical initiatives, understand the department's student-led culture, and connect with faculty mentors and student leadership.

## Positioning

A student-driven engineering community portal that connects academic foundations with real-world technical initiatives, student-led workshops, hackathons, and flagship events (such as the Meta Summit) within SLRTCE.

## Operating Context

Desktop and mobile browsers used by engineering students on campus and at home. Students browse event schedules, check committee leadership, read event overviews, and navigate to event registrations or social channels.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Confirmed Functionality:** Multi-page web architecture (Home, About, Events, Meta Summit, Team), interactive video-driven Hero with dynamic typography, motto and engineering mission showcase, recent events listing and registration entrypoints, Meta Summit flagship feature card, dedicated faculty committee and student core committee roster with profile/social links.
- **Technical Constraints:** React 19, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, GSAP animations, Lucide icons, responsive mobile-first layout, zero external paid API dependencies.
- **Undecided:** On-site registration processing backend (currently routes to external links/Google Forms).

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** ITSA (Information Technology Student Association)
- **Institution:** Shree L. R. Tiwari College of Engineering (SLRTCE)
- **Tagline:** "MAKE IT HAPPEN."
- **Brand Identity:** Deep black/dark canvas (`#08070b` / `#000000`), tech lavender accent (`#b793d2`), crisp white typography (`#ffffff`), glowing neon cyberpunk/terminal circuit cues.
- **Assets:** Official ITSA circular logo, SLRTCE institutional banner and logo, department faculty and committee photography.

## Evidence on Hand

- Verified committee and faculty roster data (`src/data/facultyData.js`, `src/data/committeeData.js`).
- Real event assets and workshop imagery in `src/assets/`.
- Site configuration details in `src/data/siteConfig.js`.

## Product Principles

1. **Student-First Clarity:** Prioritize event discovery, committee transparent leadership, and actionable participation pathways.
2. **Technical Credibility:** Demonstrate engineering craft through interactive, performance-conscious web motion and polished visual polish without unnecessary clutter.
3. **Institutional Connection:** Authentically bridge SLRTCE academic faculty mentorship with student-led initiative and community ownership.
4. **Resilient Performance:** Deliver fast, responsive performance across all student devices without relying on heavy external runtime dependencies.
